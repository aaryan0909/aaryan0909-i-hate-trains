about

What is this?

A free pocket companion for people who get scared on trains. Built for the ride itself. It works fully offline, in any metro in the world.

The not-medical-advice part

I Hate Trains is a comfort tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or replace professional help. These are coping tools, not treatment. If anxiety is running your life, please talk to a clinician you trust.

If it gets really bad

  • US or Canada: call or text 988
  • UK: call Samaritans at 116 123
  • Anywhere in an emergency: call your local emergency number
